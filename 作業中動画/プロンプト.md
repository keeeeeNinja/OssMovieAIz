| S05 | 共感④ | slow pull back revealing both women | OUT |
| S06 | 転換 | slow dolly in toward her hands and brush | IN |
| S07 | 気づき | slow drift across the products on the table | AROUND |
| S08 | 変化 | very slow push in on the eye detail | IN |
| S09 | ビフォーアフター | gentle pull back from face to upper body | OUT |
